About SavePtero

SavePtero is a browser-based survival game inspired by pterosaurs, the fascinating flying reptiles that lived millions of years ago.

The Pterosaur Inspiration

Pterosaurs were flying reptiles that lived during the Mesozoic Era. Although they lived alongside dinosaurs, pterosaurs were not dinosaurs. They were a separate group of reptiles that evolved the ability to fly.

How Did Pterosaurs Fly?

Pterosaurs had wings formed by a membrane of skin and other tissues. The wing membrane was supported by an elongated fourth finger, allowing them to fly through the air.

About the Game

In SavePtero, you control a pterosaur and try to survive as long as possible while avoiding falling meteors.

The game is designed to be simple to play and accessible directly from a web browser.
