How To Play

Objective

Help the pterosaur survive for as long as possible while avoiding falling meteors.

Controls

Use the Left Arrow and Right Arrow keys or use the A key to move the pterosaur to the left and D key to move the pterosaur to the right.

Move left or right to avoid incoming meteors and find a safe position.

Survive as Long as You Can

Your score increases as you survive. The longer you stay alive, the higher your score will become.

Increasing Difficulty

The game becomes more challenging as time passes. Meteors can become faster and more difficult to avoid.

High Score

Your best score is saved locally in your browser, allowing you to try to beat your previous record.

Game Over

The game ends when a meteor hits the pterosaur. Try again and see if you can achieve a higher score.
